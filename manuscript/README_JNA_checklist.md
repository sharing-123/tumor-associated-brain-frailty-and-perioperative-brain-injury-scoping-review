## Reference style in Zotero

In-text numbers and the bibliography in Manuscript.docx are Zotero fields. The bibliography was written in the journal format. To refresh it in Word without losing that format, first import JNA_Zotero_style.csl in Zotero (Settings, Cite, plus button) and then use Zotero, Refresh in Word. The two book chapters in the list (Cochrane Handbook chapter and the Stem Cell Oncology chapter) may differ in punctuation after a refresh and should be checked.

Citation numbers in Tables.docx and in the supplemental documents are plain text and match the manuscript numbering (tables) or the numbering inside each document (supplemental documents).
